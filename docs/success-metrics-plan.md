# The outcome

A portfolio product leader accepts most of what Juno calls ready, one decision each, and trusts the calls because each one cites its source. Ready work starts sooner and the leader stops scanning backlogs by hand.

# Metric tree

| Level | Metric | Definition | Measured by | Baseline | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| North star | Accept rate | Share of digest "ready" items the leader accepts as truly ready in one decision | Leader's accept or reject on each item, logged per digest | None yet | At least 80% (assumption) |
| Supporting | Review minutes | Leader time to review one digest | Self-report | 60 or more by hand (assumption) | Under 15 (assumption) |
| Supporting | Ready-to-started days | Days from "ready" to work beginning | Backlog dates | None yet | Falls against first run (assumption) |
| Supporting | Reversal rate | Accepted items later judged not ready | Corrections memory | None yet | Under 10% (assumption) |
| Supporting | Spike yield | Share of spikes that move an item to ready or rule it out | Spike log | None yet | At least half (assumption) |
| Guardrail, cost | Cost and time per run | Dollars and minutes, printed in the digest footer | Digest footer | Targets only | Under $2.00 and 10 minutes (PRD) |
| Guardrail, trust | Uncited "ready" items | Items shown as ready without a full citation | The separate checker | None | 0 (PRD) |

# How it feeds the evals

The accept rate is the input to the Module 5 human rubric. The rubric holds a golden set of past digest items, the leader's accept or reject on each, and a pass bar. Juno's calls are scored against that set, so the 80 percent target is tested on known answers before it is trusted on live ones.

# Position

Accept rate is the only metric allowed to stop the project. If it stays under about half after two tuning rounds (assumption), Juno's readiness judgment saves no checking, and the other four metrics do not matter.

The trust guardrail is a hard line, not a target. One uncited item shown as ready and acted on is a failure regardless of the accept rate.
