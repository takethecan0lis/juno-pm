Question 2 of 3

◷Has a date

What this is. This is about which of three candidate questions go into the one page survey sent to beta testers after their first week. You are asked now because the survey tool needs its final list by 2026-03-12.

Terms. Completion rate: the share of testers who finish the survey once they open it.

Where it came from. The product lead drafted all three on 2026-03-05. No earlier decision covers the survey.

Suggested by the product lead: the first two. They are quick to answer and tell the team what to fix first.

What changes in practice. Each added question makes the survey longer, and a longer survey tends to get fewer finished replies. Ticking all three gives more detail and a lower completion rate.

Strongest reason against the suggestion: leaving out the pricing question means the team learns nothing about willingness to pay before the April change.
Which of these three survey questions go in the beta survey?

If you stay silent: questions 1 and 2 go in the survey on 2026-03-12. Default date: 2026-03-12.
